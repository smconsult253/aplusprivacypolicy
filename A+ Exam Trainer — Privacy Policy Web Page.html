Privacy Policy — A+ Exam Trainer

Effective date: November 24, 2026

A+ Exam Trainer ("the app") is an offline study tool for the CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201) and Core 2 (220-1202) exams.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • The app does not use the internet. All questions are bundled inside the app.
  • Your study progress (questions seen, scores, and any exam in progress) is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It never leaves your device.
  • The optional "backup code" is text that the app shows to you on screen. It is only copied or shared if you choose to copy or paste it yourself.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all progress stored by the app. On Android, you can also clear the app's storage in Settings > Apps.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
